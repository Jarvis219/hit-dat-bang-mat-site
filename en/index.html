How it works

01

Phone on the floor

Screen up, right under your nose, about a hand's width from your chest. Nothing to hold.

02

Get down

Tap Get down and a 5-second countdown starts. The numbers only run once the phone is still.

03

Face down

Lower your face to the screen and come back up: one rep. The screen goes dark while you're close; Bep keeps counting out loud.

Pointless, but it really counts

"Asleep?"

Stay down for 4 seconds and you get asked; 20 seconds and it's logged as a floor nap. Too quick counts as half a rep.

Lift it, it pauses

The accelerometer knows when the phone leaves the floor. Bep crosses his arms; two still seconds and counting resumes.

Quick answers

How does it count?

With the proximity sensor at the top edge of the iPhone, the one that turns the screen off when you hold the phone to your ear. Face close switches it on, face up switches it off; each cycle is one rep.

Does it need the camera?

No. No camera, no microphone. The only permission it may ask for is notifications, and only after your first set.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. iPads have no proximity sensor, so on iPad the screen itself is the sensor: lay it face up under your face and a nose touch is a rep. Half reps, "asleep?" and the cheat stamp all still apply.

Does it cost anything?

No. Completely free, no in-app purchases, no ads. New voices unlock with the reps you do.
